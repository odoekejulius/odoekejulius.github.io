A few things worth knowing

Terms of service.

These straightforward terms apply when you browse this portfolio or contact me about web development, data analysis, or ICT work. They’re meant to set clear expectations, not to replace the written agreement for a particular project.

1. About this website

This site is a portfolio and a way to get in touch. The project descriptions, documents, and screenshots are here to show my experience. Some project materials are examples or dated snapshots, not promises that a product, dataset, or service is current or will always be available.

2. Asking about a project

You’re welcome to email or message me with a project enquiry. An enquiry is the start of a conversation, not a booking or a promise that I will take on the work. I’ll confirm availability before we agree to proceed.

3. Scope, timing, and payment

Before work begins, we’ll agree in writing on what’s included, the expected timing, the price, how payments will be made, and how feedback or changes will be handled. Work outside that scope may need a separate price or schedule. If these terms differ from a signed project agreement, the project agreement takes precedence.

4. Your materials and feedback

Please make sure you have permission to share any text, images, data, logos, or other materials you send for a project. You’re responsible for checking that the information and approvals you provide are accurate. I’ll use your materials only as needed for the work we’ve agreed.

5. Work, ownership, and portfolio examples

The project agreement will explain what you can use, what files will be handed over, and whether any third-party tools or licences apply. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, I may describe completed work or show non-confidential screenshots in my portfolio. Let me know before work starts if a project must remain private.

6. Privacy and uploaded files

This portfolio doesn’t provide a visitor file-upload service. Documents and images displayed here are published by me as public site files, so anyone with the link may be able to view or save them. Please don’t send sensitive personal, financial, health, or confidential information by email or WhatsApp unless we’ve agreed on a suitable way to handle it.

7. Third-party websites and previews

Some links or document previews may open services run by other organisations. Those services have their own availability and privacy practices, which I don’t control. If an online document preview isn’t available, use the original file link where one is provided.

8. Keeping things up to date

I do my best to keep this portfolio accurate, but details, availability, and linked services can change. Please confirm any project details directly with me before relying on them. We can update these terms when the site or services change.

9. Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you’d like to discuss a project, email odoekejulius@gmail.com.

This is a general summary for the portfolio, not legal advice or a substitute for a project-specific contract.
