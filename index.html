Profile

A broad foundation. A practical approach.

I’m Julius Chibuzo Odo-Eke, based in Abuja, Nigeria. My background brings together full-stack web development, data analysis, teaching, and a degree in Soil Science from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. That mix has taught me to look beyond the code or spreadsheet and understand the people who rely on the result.

In recent web projects, I’ve built customer-facing experiences as well as the administrative tools behind them. My analytics work includes cleaning and exploring tournament data, building Tableau-ready summaries, and checking records against their source. Earlier roles introduced me to health information systems and careful records management. I’ve also taught computer science and other subjects to secondary-school students.

I like work that is useful, clear, and maintainable. I take time to understand the brief, communicate progress, and test the details before a project is handed over.
