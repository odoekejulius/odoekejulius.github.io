Gender-group rates are close in this dataset; the smaller “Other” group should be interpreted cautiously.

The report found similar flag rates across gender and international-status groups. Nationality-group flag rates ranged from 27.3% to 29.5%.

A disparity remains by family income: the false-positive rate was 14.5% for low-income students versus 4.8% for high-income students. A risk flag should prompt a supportive, human-reviewed conversation—not a penalty.
