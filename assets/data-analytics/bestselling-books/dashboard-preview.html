International bestselling books, read as data.

Explore 7,909 monthly top-10 placements from 45 countries between June 2013 and December 2022: who is on the lists, where the authors come from, and how long books stay.

Coverage warning: only five countries (Germany, Italy, the United States, Spain and France) have a continuous monthly series, so every trend uses those five. The other 40 countries are snapshots of 1 to 10 months. Italy's list before 2018 follows a different definition and is excluded from the comparable figures. These are top-10 placements, not sales, and the results are associations rather than causes.
