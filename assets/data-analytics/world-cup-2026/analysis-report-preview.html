FIFA World Cup 2026 data analysis.

Prepared by Julius Chibuzo Odo-Eke · Report dated July 28, 2026 · Dataset snapshot through July 5, 2026

This project takes match and team tables from source data through cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, and a Tableau-ready model. The dashboard preview pairs the summary figures with group standings and confederation scoring.

Scope matters: the files contain 92 matches, covering the group stage through part of the Round of 16. The report notes that the completed 48-team tournament schedule comprises 104 matches, so this is a dated, partial snapshot—not a final-results analysis.

Cleaning and validation

The report found no duplicate match rows or orphaned team names in the supplied source tables, and checked that score and winner fields were internally consistent. Legitimate blanks were preserved where appropriate; for example, group is not applicable to knockout fixtures, and teams still active in the snapshot had no elimination round.

Comparing team elimination labels against match results revealed three status mismatches:

MexicoBlank elimination value corrected to Round of 16.
South AfricaGroup Stage corrected to Round of 32.
United StatesRound of 32 cleared; still active at the snapshot date.

The recommendation is to derive tournament status from match results instead of maintaining that field manually.

Analysis model

Data was organised around match-level results and team-level summaries to support comparisons across fixtures, teams, groups, and confederations.

Match results→Validated team status→Team summaries→Tableau exploration

Questions explored

  • How do goals and results compare across tournament stages and groups?
  • Which teams lead on points, goal difference, and attacking output in the available snapshot?
  • How do win rates and goals scored compare across confederations?
  • How can a repeatable model reduce errors in manually maintained team-status fields?

Figures and interpretations belong to the supplied project files and their July 2026 snapshot. The dashboard and report preview are static portfolio presentations; they do not connect to a live FIFA data feed.
